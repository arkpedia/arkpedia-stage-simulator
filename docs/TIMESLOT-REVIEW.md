# Timeslot source and public combat review

Timeslot (`char_4236_tmslot`) is available through the regular-stage squad and support paths with both selected source skills. The normal preview has 390 pinned source forms, 851 skills and 891 models, with 41 forms remaining. Modules, original native effects/audio and game-frame/pixel parity remain unverified. Arkpedia app dev and production remain untouched.

The foundation is 341,653 bytes, SHA-256 `8faaa4d95957d4db73907351051b8d8b5ffacf4edf22f63ee695e052afb7a7c2`, pinned to CN commit `a550f5e048bb94e7cdefc6eb97a4091f0c4c7add` and client `26-09-22-07-47-20_6c71fa`. Five original bundles retain 102 components, seven transitive templates and five projectile graphs. No referenced token or unresolved native/JSON template remains. Source audits reproduce seven outputs and reject 30 altered source/approval claims on Node 22 and 24.

Selected maximum-level raw HP/ATK/DEF are 848/222/76 at E0, 1102/285/105 at E1 and 1450/340/125 at E2. Trust adds 75 HP and 45 ATK. Base attack time is 1.3 seconds, redeployment 80 seconds, RES 10/15/20 and cost 21/23/23. P6 reduces cost by three and redeployment by ten. The visible talent adds 4%/8% ATK at E1/E2, increased by three percentage points at P5, with a 15% funnel-only Slow roll for 1.5 seconds. These talent operands remain separate from raw selected stats. The trait retains initial/delta/maximum funnel scales of .2/.15/1.1 and six stacks; attack progression/reset dispatch still needs its own runtime review.

S1 is the literal common `skcom_atk_up[3]` binding to prefab `skcom_atk_up`, rather than an invented operator-specific skill. It is manual, has thirty seconds duration and increases ATK from 30% to 100% across the ten ranks. S2 (`skchr_tmslot_2`) is a forty-second manual overload skill with one extra funnel. Its table retains overload ATK 17–50%, ASPD 20–65 and selected SP/init values. Both roots have no skill Begin animation, disallow active SP recovery, permit casting during birth and retain `canSilenced=0`. The overload root flag and event-driven overload buff remain distinct from initial mode switching; neither the art recipe nor selected-table helper invents a combat transition policy.

The owner ordinary attack uses `Attack`, original marker approximately .633333 seconds and its own projectile. S2 owner attack uses `Skill_2` and marker .5. Both use attack time, maximum animation scale one and native slow-down. Ordinary `Funnel` and the two `S2Funnel_1`/`S2Funnel_2` abilities are separate unnamed-animation clocks. S2 funnels bind different selectors, wait for projectile invalidation, fire attack-finish on invalidation and attach source mark buffs with approximately .1-second checks. Their persistent projectiles use immediate reach plus an approximately .6-second alias-bound delay and stop with the source. Ordinary owner, S2 owner and ordinary funnel projectiles instead retain speed fifteen, do not stop with source invalidity and retain trace-target final-hit behavior. These differences must survive runtime implementation.

The marked recipient checks that the source still has its S2 mode, is not already overloaded, both units live and the recipient is outside the source attack range (native overlap check), then forces overload progress. Overload creates a derived animator hook; skill finish removes mode/overload ownership. The literal source hooks replace mode-one Idle/Stun with `Skill_2_Overload_Loop`. Native compiled FSM order, enum interpretation, periodic callback timing, projectile identity and damage snapshot/RNG ownership remain unverified.

Original Front/Back clips have different overload Begin durations: one second versus approximately .833333 seconds. End is approximately .566667 and Loop is one second. Only four native mix edges exist: Skill_2/Idle to overload Loop via Begin, and overload Loop to Idle/Attack via End. They are not initial S2 cast Begin/End tracks. Common S1 retains ordinary Attack. The Back model has no death clip.
