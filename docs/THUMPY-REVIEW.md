# Thumpy source, resource and selected combat review

Thumpy (`char_4235_thumpy`) remains outside the public roster. The normal preview stays at 388 frozen source forms and 846 skills, with 43 forms remaining. Selected resources, attack/cast integration and field tile mapping are implemented. Public gameplay/browser admission and modules remain separate.

The foundation contains 467,378 bytes, SHA-256 `9a727b44984f8286d4dd8921a0c2e4e6f47e1ac62f8407bca6be0a7b421e69da`. It is pinned to CN commit `a550f5e048bb94e7cdefc6eb97a4091f0c4c7add`, client `26-09-22-07-47-20_6c71fa`. Five original bundles retain 123 owner/skill components and fifteen transitive templates. No referenced projectile/token graph or unresolved template remains.

Independent audits reproduce seven original outputs and reject 29 altered source or premature-approval claims on Node 22 and 24. Forty-three source-selection and exact-clock checks pass on both versions, covering all thirty skill ranks, promotion/potential/trust, visible and empty native talent candidates, original component identities, locked inputs and animation tracks.

The source has ordinary Attack plus three distinct Begin/Loop/Idle/End families. S1 Begin is approximately 0.166667 seconds, S2 Begin 0.666667 and S3 Begin one second; every End is approximately 0.166667. Ordinary/mode attack abilities retain native maximum animation scale `-1`, unlike Crackborne's cap of `1`; ordinary selectors also retain their block-count-related operands. These values are inputs rather than approved local timing/targeting behavior.

The first visible talent scales elemental intake and branches on a physical-output target's WATER recovery state, reducing its recovery cooldown or applying injury. The second visible talent uses source-specific recipient marks and pre-burst dispatch to grant DEF stacks and capped barriers. Empty native talent candidates remain present. S1 retains ATK/DEF and push operands; S2/S3 have separate tile setup, repeat intervals, trigger-versus-damage injury keys, and mode switching. Native stack-cap/creation dispatch and casting/FSM order remain unverified. The local resource contract described below makes its mappings explicit.

This is a stated local interpretation of selected talent behavior, not proof of compiled blackboard/creation priority; public admission remains separate.
