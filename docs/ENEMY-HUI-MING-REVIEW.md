# Hui-Ming attribute account

The Hui-Ming (yin/yang) attribute is now one shared account, and sixteen enemies are admitted through it: `enemy_1122_sphond`, `enemy_1122_sphond_2`, `enemy_1123_spsbr`, `enemy_1123_spsbr_2`, `enemy_1125_spshld`, `enemy_1125_spshld_2`, the 'Fury' pair `enemy_1129_spklr` and `enemy_1129_spklr_2`, the 'Stabby' archers `enemy_1124_spbow` and `enemy_1124_spbow_2`, the 'Blindey' pair `enemy_1127_sptrkg` and `enemy_1127_sptrkg_2`, the 'Smarty' pair `enemy_1126_spslme` and `enemy_1126_spslme_2`, and the 'Envy' pair `enemy_1128_spmage` and `enemy_1128_spmage_2`. The Hui-Ming tile controller exists as a runtime component; no stage installs it yet.

## What the source says

- **The attribute is a buff.** A unit is yin while it holds `buff_yin` and yang while it holds `buff_yang`. Both are database buffs with infinite lifetime. Starting either one finishes the other and leaves a derived presentation marker: the plain marker on a first start, a transition marker when it replaced the opposite attribute.
- **The talent chooses once.** `buff_yinyang[init]` is an immediate buff. `yinyang.dynamic` 0 starts yin, 1 starts yang, anything else starts nothing. All sixteen records use 1.
- **Two independent output scales.** `buff_yinyang[same]` and `buff_yinyang[diff]` are permanent talent buffs on the unit. On `ON_CALCULATE_DAMAGE` each checks that the damage source holds one attribute and the recipient holds the same, or the opposite, attribute, then applies `AtkScaleUp` from its own stripped blackboard: 0.6 for the same attribute and 1.4 for the opposite in every record. A recipient without an attribute is not scaled. The scale has no apply-way filter.
- **The Fury talent adds a third scale.** `enemy_spklr_t` is a permanent talent buff on the Fury pair. It multiplies output by `atkup.atk_scale` (1.8) when its owner and the recipient hold opposite attributes, on top of the 1.4, so 2.52 in total. It is carried as an optional `bonus` of the same runtime kind and stops when its own buff is removed.
- **'Stabby' prefers the opposite attribute.** Its attack is an ordinary direct ranged hit (no projectile) with radius 2.5 on ground and flying targets. Its selector lists two buff pairs, own yin with target yang and own yang with target yin, and sorts taunt last, so a candidate of the opposite attribute is chosen ahead of taunt and deployment order. The preference follows the archer's current attribute and disappears when it has none.
- **'Blindey' is blocked only by its own attribute.** The body's block condition admits a blocker only when both hold yin or both hold yang. While blocked, a checker buff on the blocker runs every tenth of a second, first after one interval, and releases the enemy when the blocker's attribute differs from the enemy's or the blocker has none.
- **'Smarty' blasts.** An Arts melee body that only a same-attribute blocker can block (no release check). It has five modes whose talents are scoped to the mode.
  - *Killed while holding an attribute:* the death talent switches to the small-blast mode for that attribute. Its three emitters release a stationary projectile that lands 0.2 s later on ground allies within 1.25: `boom.atk_scale` (4, elite 5) times ATK as Arts to recipients with no attribute, 3.6 to recipients of the blaster's attribute and 4.4 to the opposite.
  - *Meeting an opposite-attribute Smarty:* every 0.09 s each Smarty polls other holders of its `spslme_t` marker. Inside 1.5 an opposite-attribute neighbour gets a derived warning marker. Inside 1 (elite 0.8) the neighbour receives `spslme_t[boom]`: it switches to the large-blast mode at once (radius 2; scales 15, elite 18, and 13.5 / 16.5) and is killed one 0.1 s trigger later. The pair set each other off. The death talent belongs to the default mode, so no small blast follows.
  - A silenced source emits nothing. The blast action does not ask the source's own `ON_CALCULATE_DAMAGE`, so the 0.6 / 1.4 scales do not multiply it; that explicit opt-out also supports reading the event as the damage source's.
- **Hui-Ming tiles** (native tile prefabs in `battle/prefabs/[uc]tiles.ab`, inspected, not retained).
  - *Mark tiles* `tile_yinyang_road` and `tile_yinyang_wall` are dynamic buff tiles with two modes. Mode 0 gives the occupant `buff_yin`, mode 1 `buff_yang`; the stage's tile blackboard `dynamic` is the starting mode. They target the player's own ground units, and also give the occupant `buff_yinyang[same]` and `buff_yinyang[diff]` with the tile's blackboard scales (0.6 and 1.4 in the stage data) and one `locate_on_tile_yinyang` log entry. Buffs are not cleared on leaving.
  - *Switch tile* `tile_yinyang_switch` starts the immediate `buff_yinyang_switch` on a ground enemy that enters it.
- **'Envy' charged shift.** Ordinary attacks are Arts projectiles (radius 2.5, ground targets). The skill `ChargeAttack` (initial cooldown 10, cooldown 60) only selects a ground ally that holds `buff_yinyang[same]`, that is, one standing on a mark tile. It charges for the blackboard `duration` of 5 s on the `Skill` clip, then fires `projectile_enemy_spmage_s` for `atk_scale` 2 as Arts. The projectile's hit action is `SwitchDynamicBuffTileMode` `FLIP_BOOL` on the recipient's tile: the mark tile changes mode and its occupant changes attribute. A stun or silence during the charge ends it with no shot.
- `buff_yinyang_switch` swaps an existing attribute and does nothing for a unit that has none.

## What was built

- `server/sim/content/arkpedia-hui-ming-tiles.js` (`installHuiMingTiles`, `flipHuiMingTileUnder`) is the battle-level tile controller: mark tiles with a mode, occupant attribute and scales, the result log, switch tiles and the flip operation. Stage compilation must call it with the tile cells and blackboards; that wiring and the retention of the tile prefabs are stages-phase work, so until then the flip does nothing on a real stage.
- `server/sim/content/arkpedia-hui-ming-charge.js` (`huiMingChargedShift`) is the Envy component, built on the shared timed-cast scheduler and projectile transport. `attachHuiMingScales` is now shared by the enemy talent and the tiles.

- `server/sim/content/arkpedia-hui-ming-blast.js` (`huiMingBlast`) is the Smarty component: block admission pairs, the marker, the polled warning and trigger auras, the large blast with its delayed kill, and the small death blast. Damage packets can carry `skipSourceAtkScale` for native actions that do not ask the source's output scales.

- Enemy target sorting accepts a live comparator owned by a component, used for the archer's attribute-following preference. The existing paired-block component accepts several native pairs whose markers it does not own, with the release decided by the compiled native checker and an optional one-interval first delay.

- `server/sim/content/arkpedia-hui-ming.js` is the shared account: `huiMingOf`, `setHuiMing`, `switchHuiMing`. It reads and writes the unit's own `buff_yin` / `buff_yang`, for any unit kind, so future Hui-Ming tiles, devices, operators and enemy skills use the same fact. No second store exists.
- The `huiMingAttribute` runtime kind is the talent: the start-only choice and the two scales, each tied to its own attached parent buff.
- The damage pipeline gained one opt-in event, `sourceAtkScale`. It scales the pre-mitigation amount for one source and recipient pair without rewriting a `DamageInfo` shared by an area attack. Nothing changes while no component listens. Sourceless packets skip it.
- The source gate checks the talent component and binding, all five templates and both database buffs exactly, plus each body's ordinary melee packet, single mode, absence of other abilities, skeleton identity and attack clock. The guard pair's separate taunt talent is checked as exactly one permanent blackboard taunt-level addition; its behaviour stays with the existing `talent:taunt` feature.

## Verification

Thirty-nine family tests pass on raw and compiled records: start attribute and markers, 1.0 / 0.6 / 1.4 against a recipient with no, the same and the opposite attribute, independence of the two parent buffs, the three `dynamic` values, attribute replacement and switching, per-recipient isolation and refusals. The focused run with the Famiglia families, Father Agenir, the knight families (which share the `ChargeAttack` skill key), the paired-block component, the compiler, runtime plans and top-level tests that import the damage pipeline passes 538 of 539; the one failing file, `arkpedia-stage-withered-altars`, is in the committed pre-batch baseline list. The broad simulator and content run measured 1,807 tests, 1,803 passes, three skips, one todo and no failures, and all 970 pinned stage graphs pass ten-second runtime smoke. All three ran before two last one-line changes (the `dot` exclusion in the source scale and the full part list in the Envy projectile gate); the family tests and the broad run were repeated after them. The desktop and touch browser fixtures pass for all sixteen records with a deployed operator: the scales, the archer's direct hit and installed preference, same-attribute block admission and release, the Smarty death blast and two-body chain, and the Envy charge flipping an explicitly installed mark tile under the operator, with selected loading and no source catalogue requests. All sixteen original bodies decode 88 clips and render in both galleries. The Fury pair was added after the broad run; its change is confined to the Hui-Ming module, gate, registration and tests, and the focused run was repeated.

Coverage reaches 785 of 2,163 enemies, 851 database levels and 217 runtime kinds.

## Boundaries

- Operators only get an attribute in the fixtures because the test sets it. The Hui-Ming tiles and devices of the original stages are stages-phase work; no original stage is certified.
- `ON_CALCULATE_DAMAGE` is read as the damage source's event: the scale applies to damage the unit deals, not to damage it receives. That reading matches the templates' explicit source check but was not established from method bodies.
- The derived presentation marker is removed with its attribute buff. The native descriptor marks it derived without the finish-with-parent flag; which of the two governs was not established. It is presentation only.
- Five further records with `talent:yinyang` remain unadmitted: the 'Free' boss, and `enemy_15008_dqspsb` and `enemy_15082_dqklr`, which carry extra `Trace` and `TargetSelector` components of another mode and were not gated.
- The archer's `Attack_Up` and `Attack_Down` clips for targets on other heights, its selector's abnormal-flag and secondary-filter values, and the 'Blindey' visual passive `sptrkg_passive[effect]` are gated but not reproduced.
- Smarty: the five modes are gated in order by name, and the retained evidence proves which emitters exist (two small and two large per recipient class), but which mode each emitter belongs to was read from the prefab hierarchy during inspection and is not gated. The blast uses the release-time ATK and position, the delayed kill is uncredited, a blast release on a unit already detonating is ignored, and blast recipients are ground allies by centre distance, including stealthed ones (the native area ignores camouflage) but not target-free ones. The record marks Smarty silence-immune, so the silence refusal is only reachable through a forced flag.
- Tiles: the controller follows the inspected tile prefabs, whose component hashes are recorded in the receipt, but those prefabs are not retained evidence and no stage is compiled with them. The tile gives the attribute through the shared account's start (which finishes the opposite marker); the native tile uses its own inert marker buffs. Occupancy is a tenth-second scan that also re-asserts the tile's attribute if the occupant lost it, where the prefab only acts on entry and on a mode switch; enemies are located by nearest cell. A mode switch does not touch enemies on the tile (`_applyToEnemyWhenSwitchMode` is 0). The per-recipient source scale skips sourceless, unmodified and fixed-value `dot` packets.
- Envy: the charged shot deals its damage and then flips the tile, so it lands with the pre-flip relation (2 × 1.4 against an opposite-attribute occupant); the native order of damage and hit action was not established, and the reverse would give 2 × 0.6. The charge is read as lasting exactly the blackboard duration with the Skill clip and its release marker stretched to it (release at about 3.37 s); the cooldown runs from the cast start; the skill projectile's one-tile hit box is treated as its single captured target. None of these was established from method bodies.
- Effects, audio and frame/pixel parity are not reviewed.
