## Combat mapping

One nearest eligible ground target life is captured in the source `x-1` diamond at deployment. Stable native-priority ties, capture time and Start_1/Start_2/FSM choice are explicit local mappings because the compiled custom ability has no serialized implementation body. With a captured target, original Start_1 precedes Skill_Loop and Skill_End; without one, original Start_2 completes without a late passive search. Startup protection lasts until this mapped mode exit. A lost target never becomes another enemy.

Five original Skill_Loop markers apply live-ATK Arts damage using the potential's `atk_scale`. Marker six creates the nonderived, one-second recipient child with one trigger after half a second; it uses `final_atk_scale` and then requests enemy fear. The six original marker times are approximately 0, .3, .6, .9, 1.2 and 1.7 in the exact 2.200000047683716-second loop. The final hit therefore occurs at the separate delayed child trigger, not immediately at marker six. The passive's fixed clocks ignore ASPD and slow_down.

The original parent and final child are non-missable, and both damage nodes ignore the MISS cancellation reason. The fear database entry separately retains `isDamageMissable=true`. This adapter maps fear to that already accepted non-missable final invocation, without adding an independent dodge roll; it does not claim recovery of compiled VerifyAbilityDamageMiss. Existing fear immunity, Resist and movement apply. Owned fear can outlive the source and expires independently. Source/recipient replacement or callbacks cannot transfer old child/fear resources to a later life or erase foreign fear.

Ordinary attacks use two original Attack markers, approximately .466667/.666667 within 1.2999999523162842 seconds, on one captured target and one attack receipt. Each Physical hit uses live ATK, DEF and normal dodge; the second cannot become splash, another target or another attack-SP receipt. Input-time interval playback is uncapped, with live eligible slow_down applied once. Existing action-stop epochs cancel pending ordinary hits and preserve the engine's positive cooldown/restart policy. No active skill, SP bar or ready icon is fabricated.
