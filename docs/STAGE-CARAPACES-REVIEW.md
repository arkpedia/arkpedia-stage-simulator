# Exocellular Depositor and Carapace platforms

Exocellular Depositor now creates the original level-one Excrescent Carapace
when defeated on an eligible tile. The spawn requires passable low ground with
ordinary movement cost, no occupied placed body, reservation or downed operator,
and excludes start, exit and teleport tiles. Source exit creates nothing.
The existing native outcome auxiliary accounts for its killed counter.

One shared Carapace definition serves both death spawns and original predefines.
It interpolates the retained thirty device levels: level one has 3,000 HP and
320 DEF; level thirty has 5,000 HP and 100 DEF. Both have twenty block, zero slot
cost and the original status immunities. Target-free, ally-target-free and
heal-free are projected into the existing selection/healing rules. A blocked
enemy can still attack the platform through the core blocker-priority path.

The reusable platform service owns a ranged build override, elevation and one
host/guest tile account. The host retains occupancy, redirects routes through
the existing obstacle-cost pathfinder and prevents its guest from blocking.
Destroying the host restores terrain and defeats the occupant through the core
kill path. Rebirth remains possible, with the surviving guest taking over the
ordinary tile account. Independent platform and water-surface state is retained.

The last overlapping operator is retained as the native ref buff's card source.
Host defeat grants a -35% redeploy-time card ratio until that card next deploys.
The card service also adjusts an already-running cooldown from its recorded
removal time. It does not modify later deployments, normal DP cost increases,
other deployment locks or permanent squad removal. Overlap events appear in
stage outcomes as dsshell_overlapped.

The original .5-second Nethersea Brand removal action is retained and wired to
the future Nethersea controller's clearTile interface. Brand propagation and
its controller remain separate unimplemented stage capabilities; this adapter
does not manufacture their state or grant admission to those stages. Exact
compiled ordering, additive card-attribute buckets, host geometry and original
client-frame parity are not certified.

The original Depositor body passes five clip checks on desktop and mobile, and
its desktop gallery was inspected. Carapace uses a Unity mesh/Animation body,
not a Spine skeleton. The Spine importer correctly refused it; original mesh
export/rendering remains pending and the existing labelled fallback actor is
used. No original Carapace body rendering approval is claimed.

Eight focused cases cover source tile restrictions, stats, occupancy, immunity,
rebirth, real ranged deployment, one-use card ratios and original BP-2's complete
wave schedule under controlled kills. The combined suite passes 274 tests.
The 926 compiled stages pass ten-second runtime smoke checks. The device store
contains thirteen families and 304 definitions. Enemy support is 695 of 2,163.
