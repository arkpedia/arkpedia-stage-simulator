## Chain healing

One accepted heal receipt emits a speed-ten chain with a ten-second lifetime. The source nine-cell `x-4` range is centred on the current receiver. Later hops can select full-health allies and travel outside the caster's original range; the same entity is never visited twice. The selected order is lowest HP ratio, then most recent deployment. Ordinary initial acquisition requires missing HP; S2's marker-time selection also permits full HP. The selector enum aliases remain local mappings.

The base three-receiver chain uses its table's .75 decay, not the projectile's raw .85 fallback or a module. The two original marker validators are independent and ignore marker source. An accepted `atk_scale_up` marker multiplies the current projectile scale by the selected talent coefficient; that multiplier carries into subsequent hops. The accepted `free_jump` marker adds one bounce and suppresses decay on the *next* hop, even if the marked receiver was full. The marked receiver's arriving hop still has its previous decay. At E2/P1, illustrative multiplier sequences are `1.2, 1.2, .9, .675` when Ukusik is first, and `1, .9, .9, .675` when second. Accepted native markers on another selected Ukusik also qualify; a name or an unaccepted talent does not.

The original markers, validator pointers and blackboard keys are primary inputs. The mechanism authors' [firsthand ChainLightningHitBehaviour analysis](https://prts.wiki/w/话题:Zkfsc0tkcmgr7a8s) (2026-08-28, 调零修罗 and Enko) corroborates the carried multiplier and next-hop suppression. This community analysis does not establish matching compiled-client provenance or native frame parity.

## Skills and resource ownership

S1 starts its skill account at activation, plays the original Begin, then independently admits its ASPD and mode buffs for the remaining account. Accepted mode one uses Skill_1_Loop/Idle and one additional chain receiver. A rejected mode cannot grant extra jumps; a rejected ASPD buff cannot grant speed. Dispel restores ordinary mode; positive cooldown is preserved. The original End mix returns to Idle. Account/Begin/buff timing remains an explicit local mapping.

S2 loses the selected rank's fraction of current HP without killing the caster, bypassing shields/modifiers and attack SP. At the original marker it chooses the current recipient and launches the one-shot chain. The original cast holds SP through its 1.5-second window. Empty attempts do not fabricate a friendly receiver. Self-cost event 2, cast bookkeeping, source-invalid receiver attachment and numeric override type 3 remain explicit local mappings.

Every S2 receiver independently gets one-second ATK-snapshot healing and a .1-second camouflage checker for its rank duration. Healing is the original AdvancedApplyHeal path: it respects HealFree and ordinary healing modifiers; it is not an HP-regeneration attribute. It survives caster retirement with its own finite snapshot/timer. The checker is source-dependent; it starts after its first interval, removes camouflage while its receiver blocks, and can restore it on a later check. Native contains/finish actions explicitly ignore buff source for the same `turdus_s2[camouflage]` family. Separate producers keep independent healing while sharing that camouflage family; unrelated camouflage is preserved. Reapplication, vetoes, callback retirement, foreign replacements and recipient life changes cannot leave stale owned resources.

A small shared heal-pipeline guard retains the recipient life across `heal` and `calculatedHeal` callbacks. A callback that replaces/removes that life cannot transfer the old heal into its replacement. Unchanged recipient lives retain the existing modifier, credit and HP ordering.
