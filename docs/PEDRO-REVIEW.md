Pedro (`char_4234_pedro`) remains outside the public roster. The normal preview stays at 390 pinned source forms, 851 skills and 891 models, with 41 forms remaining. Source selection and original artwork do not approve combat, modules, native effects/audio or game-frame/pixel parity. Arkpedia app dev and production remain untouched.

Base attack time is 2.1 seconds; cost is 14/16/16, RES ten and base redeployment seventy seconds. The talent separately holds a requirement of two other operators in range and 5%/10% ATK at E1/E2, increased by two percentage points at P5. It is a source-specific mark/stack count and derived ATK buff, with an approximately .2-second native check; it must not become an unconditional table stat.

S1 is offensive-recovery automatic next-attack work, with SP cost eight down to four. It selects two targets, preferring those without its mark. The direct attack scale is 105–135%; the one-use mark adds 135–220% source ATK as Arts damage after its Arts-damage condition. The graph consumes the mark before the extra damage and excludes its own modifier key to prevent recursive responses. The source damage action requests cached ATK, no-source output and the original mark modifier key. Ordinary and S1 projectiles travel at speed fifteen, retain trace-target final hits and do not stop when the source becomes invalid. Exact compiled hit/mark attachment order, snapshot timing, source invalidity and damage-hook priority still require an explicit combat mapping.

S2 is manual with two charges, SP cost 34 down to 22 and initial SP 15 up to 19. It selects three allied targets, preferring those without the protection mark. The source graph compares incoming and existing heal snapshots before replacement, stores 120–210% ATK and checks an inclusive half-HP threshold immediately on attachment (`waitFirstTriggerInterval=false`), then approximately every .2 seconds. On trigger it consumes protection before applying the stored heal, then applies minus-one taunt for eight seconds. The selected skill duration is minus one; the eight-second blackboard value belongs to the post-trigger taunt, not a duration skill or temporary protection expiry. The native heal source enum is TARGET. Compiled heal attribution, snapshot comparisons, polling order, multi-source override and after-retreat lifetime remain separate review questions.
